04 — Frosted Glass (Xira shisha)
Uslub: Modern · Minimal
O‘lcham: 200 × 100 mm
Material: Matlashtirilgan (satin/frosted) akril 8 mm, qirralari polirovka qilingan. Muqobil: 6 mm xira shisha (shisha ustaxonasida). Yozuvlar — yashil UV-bosma oq podloshka bilan yoki yashil plyonka.
Ishlab chiqarish: Lazer/CNC kesish, burchaklar R3, qirra polirovka, 4 ta teshik Ø8 mm.
O‘rnatish: Xromlangan dekorativ distansion ushlagich Ø19 × 20 mm, 4 dona. Teshik markazi qirralardan 14 mm.
Shrift: Jost Medium (fayllarda matn konturga aylantirilgan)
Ranglar: #E2ECEA Xira shisha, #18744A Yashil, #2F6F55 To‘q yashil, #8FBCB0 Shisha qirrasi, #C9CED3 Xrom
Eslatma: Shisha varianti tanlansa, teshiklarni shisha ustaxonasida toblashdan (temper) oldin parmalash kerak.
